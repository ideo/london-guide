01 — The studio

A small, tight-knit team, right in the middle of it.

IDEO London is a small, tight-knit community from around the world, spread across the 5th and 6th floors of Herbal House. We work hands-on across purpose, product, brand, organisation and business model — on the knotty, systemic problems that don't have an obvious next step.

The neighbourhood suits the work: London is steeped in history but forever reinventing itself, and Clerkenwell in particular has been a home for makers, printers and craftspeople for centuries. You're a five-minute walk from Farringdon Station and surrounded by more good coffee per square foot than almost anywhere else in the city — use the rest of this guide to make the most of it.
